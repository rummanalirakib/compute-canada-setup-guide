# Registering for Compute Canada

This guide will walk you through the process of registering for Compute Canada resources. 

## Introduction
Compute Canada provides advanced research computing resources across Canada. Before using these resources, you need to create an account and register.

## Prerequisites
Before you start, ensure you have the following:
- An email address from a recognized Canadian institution (e.g., a university or research organization)
- Access to a device with internet connectivity

## Registration Steps
Follow these steps to register:
1. Visit the official [Compute Canada registration page](https://www.computecanada.ca/).
2. Click on **Register** at the top-right corner.
3. Fill out the required information:
   - Institution
   - Email Address
   - Research Group (if applicable)
4. Confirm your email via the verification link sent to your inbox.
5. Complete your profile by adding the requested details.

For more detailed information on each step, refer to the provided [PDF guide](./your-guide.pdf).

## Troubleshooting
If you encounter issues during registration:
- Ensure your email address is from a recognized institution.
- Check your spam/junk folder for the confirmation email.
- Clear your browser cache or try a different browser.

## Further Assistance
If you need further assistance, you can contact Compute Canada support via:
- Email: support@computecanada.ca
- Support Portal: [Compute Canada Support](https://support.computecanada.ca)
